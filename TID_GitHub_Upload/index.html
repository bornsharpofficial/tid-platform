Investment Workflow
01Identify parcelsWI SCO Parcel Map → download county shapefile → overlay TID boundary to extract every parcel within the district
02Build owner listPropWire → search by TID city/zip → filter absentee owners, vacant properties, high equity → export CSV at no cost
03Source listingsLoopNet for commercial & industrial · Zillow for residential · LandWatch for vacant land · cross-reference by municipality
04Resolve entitiesWI DFI Business Search → identify the decision-maker behind LLCs and trusts holding commercial TID parcels
05Get contactsBatchSkipTracing.com → upload owner CSV → retrieve verified phone & email → $0.10 per record, no subscription
06Log & investigateCheck the Investigate box in Project Tracker or TID Database → auto-flags on map · adds to Flagged TIDs · syncs to team
